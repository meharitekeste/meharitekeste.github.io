Testing services

Single-purpose projects that use standard processes or procedures according to ASTM and ASABE standards. Longer projects can be internal, government-sponsored or commercially sponsored.

Soil mechanics testing

  • Gravimetric material moisture content
  • Atterberg limits of soil (plastic, liquid)
  • Moisture density curve (Proctor mold test)
  • Particle distribution using sieve method
  • Shear strength tests (direct shear and triaxial tests)

Soil compaction and contact

  • Soil compaction measurement (soil bulk density and ASABE standard cone penetration testing)
  • 2D and 3D tire/track-to-soil footprint

Test soils

  • Artificial soil, Clarion series loam soil, sandy soil, clay soil (60% clay fraction), pea gravel and limestone gravel
  • Able to create simulants, or bring your own
2025 report

Tire–artificial soil contact properties from VF tires of a self-propelled sprayer

Standardized vehicle load and tire footprint data for self-propelled sprayers are currently lacking. Using Iowa State's soil bin footprint method, at 83 kPa the Alliance VF750/60R46 tire (57 kN load) displayed a 5.2% larger footprint than the Goodyear LSW 800/55R46, resulting in a 3.7% reduction in soil rut depth. At 117 kPa (17 psi), the Alliance tire had a 3.2% smaller contact area. Reducing tire pressure from 117 kPa to 83 kPa led to shallower rut depths of 7.3% (Alliance) and 6.9% (Goodyear).
